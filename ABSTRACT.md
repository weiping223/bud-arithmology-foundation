# 摘要（中英双语）

## 中文

本报告提出一种“集数观”：将数视为带类型、深度与投影接口的集合构造。
点胚 ϑ 不是空集，而是带有内部拓扑或两叶结构的原始对象；
其内部等式 0 ≃ 1 被严格限制在胚层，不能穿越投影 π 进入经典算术。
自然数仍由 von Neumann 构造生成，经典 Peano 算术在投影下完整复位。
生成深度 deg 作为胚层不变量被引入，但不伪装成基数或序数。
素数理论被明确留作接口，而非由胚直接定义。

本工作不构成对 ZF 一致性的证明，也不提供素数新公式，
而是给出一套可形式化检验的结构性纲领。

## English

This draft proposes a "set-number" view: numbers are typed sets
equipped with generation depth and a projection interface.

The germ ϑ is not the empty set, but a pre-set with internal topology
or a two-leaf (Möbius-like) structure. Internal equality `0 ≃ 1`
is strictly confined to the germ layer and cannot cross the projection π
into classical arithmetic.

Natural numbers are still generated by the von Neumann construction,
and classical Peano arithmetic is fully restored under projection.
Generation depth `deg` is introduced as a germ-level invariant,
distinct from cardinality or ordinality.

Prime theory is explicitly left as an interface, not defined by the germ.

This work does **not** prove relative consistency over ZF,
nor does it provide a new prime formula;
it offers a structurally explicit framework for scrutiny.
